# 001 — c0/c1 lose live to the real-bot market (place 5–6/6)

**Adversary:** the live lobby field (QuertyBot, Lbender, SevenUp, N1gel, Jaeger, RackMaw-Blend25, rackpilot-live, StickBot, …).

## What happened (matches 45629, 45640, 45721, 45733)
| | dumpling (c0/c1) | top-3 bots (avg) |
|---|---|---|
| tiles won | 2 | 14–20 |
| coins spent | 21–31 | 160–220 |
| avg price/tile | ~11 | 10–12 |
| final balance | 89–109 | 1–31 |
| words | 1 (end liquidation) | 3–4, 5–8 letters, first at 7–17 s |
| score | 17–33 | 80–132 |

Our bids on 1-point tiles were 2 (c0) and 7–8 (c1); clearing prices were 6–16. The median loss margin in c1 was 3–5 coins; we sat on ~80 coins for 40+ rounds.

## Root cause
1. **Valuation anchored on face value.** κ·face option value prices a vowel at ~2 points, but in a competitive market a vowel's real value is its *enabling* value in 7-letter words (~5–7 points/tile observed: KEYCARD 51/7, ADJUVANT 57/8).
2. **Money was valued as if it had terminal value.** Unspent balance is worth 0 at match end. A fixed money multiplier μ can't adapt to a market 2–3× pricier than the one we tuned against.
3. **Sim market was mis-calibrated** (the zoo had face-value bidders only).

## Fix (principled, not adversary-specific)
- Per-tile enabling value τ in the partition objective (c1).
- **Adaptive score-per-coin threshold λ** (c2): fit clearing price ≈ a + b·face from the public `paid` stream; choose λ so expected spend over remaining auctions on tiles with value/price ≥ λ equals effective budget (balance × (1+inc)); bid value/λ (Vickrey-truthful at the shadow price of money).
- Live-calibrated `marketLive` rival in the zoo; tuning on three fields incl. it.

## Result
c2 first live match (45822): 14 tiles for 154 coins (on par with the field), median loss margin 1 coin, score 70, place 4/6. Remaining gap is tile→score conversion, addressed next.
